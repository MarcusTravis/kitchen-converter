      Low300–325150–1652–3Rich fruit cakes, custards, cheesecake
      Moderate350–375175–1904–5Most cakes, cookies and casseroles
      Moderately hot4002056Muffins, roast vegetables

      Very hot475–500245–2609–10Pizza on a stone, quick high-heat roasting
