Tips for switching pans

  • Same area, same time. When the batter sits at the same depth, the bake time stays close to the recipe’s.
  • Deeper batter bakes longer. In a smaller pan, lower the oven by about 25 °F and check later than the recipe says. In a larger, shallower pan, start checking 5 to 10 minutes early.
  • Glass and dark metal run hotter. Many bakers drop the oven 25 °F for glass or dark nonstick pans.
  • Bundt and tube pans are best matched by how many cups of water they hold, not by diameter.
  • Doneness beats the clock. A toothpick in the center should come out clean or with a few moist crumbs.
