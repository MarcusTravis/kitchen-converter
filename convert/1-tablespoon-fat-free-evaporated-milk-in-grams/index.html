How many grams is 1 tablespoon of evaporated milk (fat-free)?

Answer

16 g

1 tablespoon of evaporated milk (fat-free) is about 16 grams, or 0.56 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 256 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

A 12-ounce can holds 1½ cups. Fat-free evaporated milk can stand in for regular evaporated milk in most pies and casseroles.

Measuring tip

Liquids are the easiest thing to measure by volume, so cups and a scale will agree closely. Dairy weighs a little more or less than water depending on its fat, sugar and air content.
