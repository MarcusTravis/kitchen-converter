How many grams is ½ teaspoon of ground cloves?

Answer

1.1 g

½ teaspoon of ground cloves is about 1.1 grams, or 0.04 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 104 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

Small amounts like these are hard to read on a regular kitchen scale. A pocket scale that reads to 0.1 g helps, or just use level measuring spoons.
