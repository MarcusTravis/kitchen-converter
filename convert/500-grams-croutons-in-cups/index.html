How many cups is 500 grams of croutons / dry bread cubes (plain)?

Answer

16 ⅔ cups

500 grams of croutons / dry bread cubes (plain) fills 16 ⅔ cups (16.7 US cups, or about 267 tablespoons). The math uses USDA's figure of roughly 30 g per cup.

Use this for dry, unseasoned bread cubes like the ones in a bag of stuffing cubes. Seasoned cubes weigh a little more per cup.

Measuring tip

Dry crumbs and cubes are mostly air, so a cup weighs very little and pressing down changes the amount a lot. Fill the cup loosely unless the recipe says packed, or go by weight from the bag. When a recipe gives grams, a scale is always the most accurate choice.
