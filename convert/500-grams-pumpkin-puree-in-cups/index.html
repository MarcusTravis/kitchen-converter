How many cups is 500 grams of pumpkin purée (canned)?

Answer

≈ 2 cups

500 grams of pumpkin purée (canned) fills about 2 cups (2.04 US cups, or about 32.7 tablespoons). The math uses USDA's figure of roughly 245 g per cup.

This is plain canned pumpkin, not pumpkin pie filling (which already has sugar and spice in it). A standard 15-ounce can holds about 425 g, which is roughly 1¾ cups.

Measuring tip

Purées and sauces settle into the cup with little air, so they measure fairly consistently. Scrape the cup out with a spatula, and for whole fruit like fresh cranberries, a heaping or shaken-down cup can change the weight noticeably. When a recipe gives grams, a scale is always the most accurate choice.
