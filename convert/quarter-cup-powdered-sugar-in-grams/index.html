How many grams is ¼ cup of powdered sugar (unsifted)?

Answer

30 g

¼ cup of powdered sugar (unsifted) is about 30 grams, or 1.1 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 120 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

This is for unsifted powdered sugar. Sifting adds air, so a cup of sifted sugar weighs less.

Measuring tip

For sticky syrups, give the cup a light coat of oil first so everything slides out and you get the full amount. Brown sugar is the one sugar that is normally packed firmly into the cup.
