How many grams is 1 cup of whole milk?

Answer

244 g

1 cup of whole milk is about 244 grams, or 8.6 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 244 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

Liquids are the easiest thing to measure by volume, so cups and a scale will agree closely. Dairy weighs a little more or less than water depending on its fat, sugar and air content.
