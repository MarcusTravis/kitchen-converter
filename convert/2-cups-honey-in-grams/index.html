How many grams is 2 cups of honey?

Answer

678 g

2 cups of honey is about 678 grams, or 23.9 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 339 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

For sticky syrups, give the cup a light coat of oil first so everything slides out and you get the full amount. Brown sugar is the one sugar that is normally packed firmly into the cup.
