How many grams is 1 tablespoon of raisins?

Answer

9.1 g

1 tablespoon of raisins is about 9.1 grams, or 0.32 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 145 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

Whole, chopped and sliced pieces leave different amounts of air in the cup, so the same nut can weigh quite differently depending on the cut. Pick the closest match.
