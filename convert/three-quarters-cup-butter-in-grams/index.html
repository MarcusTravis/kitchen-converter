How many grams is ¾ cup of butter?

Answer

170 g

¾ cup of butter is about 170 grams, or 6 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 227 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

In the US, one stick of butter is ½ cup or 8 tablespoons, which is about 113 g (USDA rounds a cup to 227 g, so the chart shows 114 g).

Measuring tip

Solid fats are easiest to measure by weight or with the markings on the wrapper. Oils and melted fats can go straight into a liquid measuring cup.
