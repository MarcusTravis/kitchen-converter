How many grams is 2 cups of coconut oil?

Answer

436 g

2 cups of coconut oil is about 436 grams, or 15.4 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 218 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

Solid fats are easiest to measure by weight or with the markings on the wrapper. Oils and melted fats can go straight into a liquid measuring cup.
