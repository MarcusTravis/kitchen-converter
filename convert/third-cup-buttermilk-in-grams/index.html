How many grams is ⅓ cup of buttermilk?

Answer

82 g

⅓ cup of buttermilk is about 82 grams, or 2.9 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 245 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

Liquids are the easiest thing to measure by volume, so cups and a scale will agree closely. Dairy weighs a little more or less than water depending on its fat, sugar and air content.
