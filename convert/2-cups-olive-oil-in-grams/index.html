How many grams is 2 cups of olive oil?

Answer

432 g

2 cups of olive oil is about 432 grams, or 15.2 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 216 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

Solid fats are easiest to measure by weight or with the markings on the wrapper. Oils and melted fats can go straight into a liquid measuring cup.
