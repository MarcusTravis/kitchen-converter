How many grams is 1 cup of cream cheese?

Answer

232 g

1 cup of cream cheese is about 232 grams, or 8.2 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 232 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

Liquids are the easiest thing to measure by volume, so cups and a scale will agree closely. Dairy weighs a little more or less than water depending on its fat, sugar and air content.
