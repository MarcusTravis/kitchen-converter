How many cups is 250 grams of evaporated milk?

Answer

≈ 1 cup

250 grams of evaporated milk fills about 1 cup (0.992 US cups, or about 15.9 tablespoons). The math uses USDA's figure of roughly 252 g per cup.

Evaporated milk is milk with about 60% of the water cooked off. It is not the same as sweetened condensed milk, which is much thicker and very sweet. A 12-ounce can holds 1½ cups.

Measuring tip

Liquids are the easiest thing to measure by volume, so cups and a scale will agree closely. Dairy weighs a little more or less than water depending on its fat, sugar and air content. When a recipe gives grams, a scale is always the most accurate choice.
