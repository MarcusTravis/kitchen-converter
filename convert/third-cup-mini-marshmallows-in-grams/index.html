How many grams is ⅓ cup of mini marshmallows?

Answer

17 g

⅓ cup of mini marshmallows is about 17 grams, or 0.59 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 50 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

Small amounts like these are hard to read on a regular kitchen scale. A pocket scale that reads to 0.1 g helps, or just use level measuring spoons.
