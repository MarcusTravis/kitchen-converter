How many grams is 1 tablespoon of plain yogurt?

Answer

15 g

1 tablespoon of plain yogurt is about 15 grams, or 0.54 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 245 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

Liquids are the easiest thing to measure by volume, so cups and a scale will agree closely. Dairy weighs a little more or less than water depending on its fat, sugar and air content.
