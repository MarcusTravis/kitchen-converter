How many cups is 100 grams of chocolate chips (semisweet)?

Answer

≈ ⅝ cup

100 grams of chocolate chips (semisweet) fills about ⅝ cup (0.595 US cups, or about 9.52 tablespoons). The math uses USDA's figure of roughly 168 g per cup.

Measuring tip

Small amounts like these are hard to read on a regular kitchen scale. A pocket scale that reads to 0.1 g helps, or just use level measuring spoons. When a recipe gives grams, a scale is always the most accurate choice.
