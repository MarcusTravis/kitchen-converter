How many cups is 100 grams of almonds (sliced)?

Answer

≈ 1 ⅛ cups

100 grams of almonds (sliced) fills about 1 ⅛ cups (1.09 US cups, or about 17.4 tablespoons). The math uses USDA's figure of roughly 92 g per cup.

Measuring tip

Whole, chopped and sliced pieces leave different amounts of air in the cup, so the same nut can weigh quite differently depending on the cut. Pick the closest match. When a recipe gives grams, a scale is always the most accurate choice.
