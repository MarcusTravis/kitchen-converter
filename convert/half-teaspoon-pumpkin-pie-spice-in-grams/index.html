How many grams is ½ teaspoon of pumpkin pie spice?

Answer

0.9 g

½ teaspoon of pumpkin pie spice is about 0.9 grams, or 0.03 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 89.6 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

Small amounts like these are hard to read on a regular kitchen scale. A pocket scale that reads to 0.1 g helps, or just use level measuring spoons.
