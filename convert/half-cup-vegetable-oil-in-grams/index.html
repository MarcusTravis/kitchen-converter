How many grams is ½ cup of vegetable (canola) oil?

Answer

109 g

½ cup of vegetable (canola) oil is about 109 grams, or 3.8 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 218 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

Solid fats are easiest to measure by weight or with the markings on the wrapper. Oils and melted fats can go straight into a liquid measuring cup.
