How many grams is ½ cup of almonds (whole)?

Answer

72 g

½ cup of almonds (whole) is about 72 grams, or 2.5 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 143 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

Whole, chopped and sliced pieces leave different amounts of air in the cup, so the same nut can weigh quite differently depending on the cut. Pick the closest match.
