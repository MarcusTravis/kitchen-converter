How many cups is 500 grams of whole wheat flour?

Answer

≈ 4 ⅛ cups

500 grams of whole wheat flour fills about 4 ⅛ cups (4.17 US cups, or about 66.7 tablespoons). The math uses USDA's figure of roughly 120 g per cup.

Measuring tip

Flour packs down as it sits, so a cup scooped straight from the bag can weigh noticeably more than one that was spooned in and leveled with a knife. Spoon and level for results closest to these numbers, or better, weigh it. When a recipe gives grams, a scale is always the most accurate choice.
