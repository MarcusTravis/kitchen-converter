How many grams is ½ teaspoon of table salt?

Answer

3 g

½ teaspoon of table salt is about 3 grams, or 0.11 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 292 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

This is fine table salt. Kosher and flaky salts have bigger crystals and weigh much less per spoon, so do not swap them one for one.

Measuring tip

Small amounts like these are hard to read on a regular kitchen scale. A pocket scale that reads to 0.1 g helps, or just use level measuring spoons.
