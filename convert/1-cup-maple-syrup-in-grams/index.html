How many grams is 1 cup of maple syrup?

Answer

315 g

1 cup of maple syrup is about 315 grams, or 11.1 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 315 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

For sticky syrups, give the cup a light coat of oil first so everything slides out and you get the full amount. Brown sugar is the one sugar that is normally packed firmly into the cup.
