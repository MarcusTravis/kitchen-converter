How many grams is ⅔ cup of rolled oats?

Answer

54 g

⅔ cup of rolled oats is about 54 grams, or 1.9 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 81 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

These figures are for dry, uncooked grains and legumes. Once cooked they soak up water and a cup weighs very differently.
