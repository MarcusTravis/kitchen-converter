How many grams is 1 cup of pumpkin pie filling (canned, spiced)?

Answer

270 g

1 cup of pumpkin pie filling (canned, spiced) is about 270 grams, or 9.5 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 270 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Pie filling is pumpkin with sugar and spices already added. If your recipe calls for pumpkin purée, use plain canned pumpkin instead.

Measuring tip

Purées and sauces settle into the cup with little air, so they measure fairly consistently. Scrape the cup out with a spatula, and for whole fruit like fresh cranberries, a heaping or shaken-down cup can change the weight noticeably.
