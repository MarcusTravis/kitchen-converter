How many grams is 1 cup of shredded coconut (sweetened)?

Answer

93 g

1 cup of shredded coconut (sweetened) is about 93 grams, or 3.3 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 93 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

Small amounts like these are hard to read on a regular kitchen scale. A pocket scale that reads to 0.1 g helps, or just use level measuring spoons.
