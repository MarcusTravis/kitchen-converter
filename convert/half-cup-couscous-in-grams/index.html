How many grams is ½ cup of couscous (dry)?

Answer

87 g

½ cup of couscous (dry) is about 87 grams, or 3.1 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 173 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

These figures are for dry, uncooked grains and legumes. Once cooked they soak up water and a cup weighs very differently.
