How many grams is 1 cup of bread crumbs (dry, seasoned)?

Answer

120 g

1 cup of bread crumbs (dry, seasoned) is about 120 grams, or 4.2 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 120 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

Dry crumbs and cubes are mostly air, so a cup weighs very little and pressing down changes the amount a lot. Fill the cup loosely unless the recipe says packed, or go by weight from the bag.
