How many grams is 1 teaspoon of ground cinnamon?

Answer

2.6 g

1 teaspoon of ground cinnamon is about 2.6 grams, or 0.09 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 124.8 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

Small amounts like these are hard to read on a regular kitchen scale. A pocket scale that reads to 0.1 g helps, or just use level measuring spoons.
