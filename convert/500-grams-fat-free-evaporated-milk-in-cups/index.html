How many cups is 500 grams of evaporated milk (fat-free)?

Answer

≈ 2 cups

500 grams of evaporated milk (fat-free) fills about 2 cups (1.95 US cups, or about 31.3 tablespoons). The math uses USDA's figure of roughly 256 g per cup.

A 12-ounce can holds 1½ cups. Fat-free evaporated milk can stand in for regular evaporated milk in most pies and casseroles.

Measuring tip

Liquids are the easiest thing to measure by volume, so cups and a scale will agree closely. Dairy weighs a little more or less than water depending on its fat, sugar and air content. When a recipe gives grams, a scale is always the most accurate choice.
