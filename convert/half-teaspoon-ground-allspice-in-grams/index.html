How many grams is ½ teaspoon of ground allspice?

Answer

1 g

½ teaspoon of ground allspice is about 1 grams, or 0.04 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 96 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

Small amounts like these are hard to read on a regular kitchen scale. A pocket scale that reads to 0.1 g helps, or just use level measuring spoons.
