How many grams is 1 tablespoon of cake flour?

Answer

8.6 g

1 tablespoon of cake flour is about 8.6 grams, or 0.3 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 137 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

This assumes unsifted cake flour dipped into the cup. Sifted flour weighs less per cup.

Measuring tip

Flour packs down as it sits, so a cup scooped straight from the bag can weigh noticeably more than one that was spooned in and leveled with a knife. Spoon and level for results closest to these numbers, or better, weigh it.
