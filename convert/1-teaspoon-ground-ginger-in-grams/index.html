How many grams is 1 teaspoon of ground ginger?

Answer

1.7 g

1 teaspoon of ground ginger is about 1.7 grams, or 0.06 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 83.2 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

Small amounts like these are hard to read on a regular kitchen scale. A pocket scale that reads to 0.1 g helps, or just use level measuring spoons.
