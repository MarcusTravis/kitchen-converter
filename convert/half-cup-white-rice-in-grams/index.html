How many grams is ½ cup of white rice (long-grain, uncooked)?

Answer

93 g

½ cup of white rice (long-grain, uncooked) is about 93 grams, or 3.3 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 185 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

These figures are for dry, uncooked grains and legumes. Once cooked they soak up water and a cup weighs very differently.
