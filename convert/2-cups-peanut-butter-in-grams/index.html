How many grams is 2 cups of peanut butter (smooth)?

Answer

516 g

2 cups of peanut butter (smooth) is about 516 grams, or 18.2 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 258 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

Solid fats are easiest to measure by weight or with the markings on the wrapper. Oils and melted fats can go straight into a liquid measuring cup.
