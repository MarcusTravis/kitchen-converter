How many grams is ½ cup of light corn syrup?

Answer

171 g

½ cup of light corn syrup is about 171 grams, or 6 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 341 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

For sticky syrups, give the cup a light coat of oil first so everything slides out and you get the full amount. Brown sugar is the one sugar that is normally packed firmly into the cup.
