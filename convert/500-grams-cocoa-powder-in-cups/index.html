How many cups is 500 grams of cocoa powder (unsweetened)?

Answer

≈ 5 ⅞ cups

500 grams of cocoa powder (unsweetened) fills about 5 ⅞ cups (5.81 US cups, or about 93 tablespoons). The math uses USDA's figure of roughly 86 g per cup.

Measuring tip

Small amounts like these are hard to read on a regular kitchen scale. A pocket scale that reads to 0.1 g helps, or just use level measuring spoons. When a recipe gives grams, a scale is always the most accurate choice.
