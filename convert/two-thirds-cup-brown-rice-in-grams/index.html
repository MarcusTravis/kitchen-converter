How many grams is ⅔ cup of brown rice (uncooked)?

Answer

123 g

⅔ cup of brown rice (uncooked) is about 123 grams, or 4.4 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 185 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

These figures are for dry, uncooked grains and legumes. Once cooked they soak up water and a cup weighs very differently.
