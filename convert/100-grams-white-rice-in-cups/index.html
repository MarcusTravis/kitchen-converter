How many cups is 100 grams of white rice (long-grain, uncooked)?

Answer

≈ ½ cup

100 grams of white rice (long-grain, uncooked) fills about ½ cup (0.541 US cups, or about 8.65 tablespoons). The math uses USDA's figure of roughly 185 g per cup.

Measuring tip

These figures are for dry, uncooked grains and legumes. Once cooked they soak up water and a cup weighs very differently. When a recipe gives grams, a scale is always the most accurate choice.
