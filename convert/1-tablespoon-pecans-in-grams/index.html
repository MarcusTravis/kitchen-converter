How many grams is 1 tablespoon of pecans (chopped)?

Answer

6.8 g

1 tablespoon of pecans (chopped) is about 6.8 grams, or 0.24 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 109 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

Whole, chopped and sliced pieces leave different amounts of air in the cup, so the same nut can weigh quite differently depending on the cut. Pick the closest match.
