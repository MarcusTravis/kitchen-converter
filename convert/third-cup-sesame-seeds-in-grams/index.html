How many grams is ⅓ cup of sesame seeds?

Answer

48 g

⅓ cup of sesame seeds is about 48 grams, or 1.7 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 144 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

Whole, chopped and sliced pieces leave different amounts of air in the cup, so the same nut can weigh quite differently depending on the cut. Pick the closest match.
