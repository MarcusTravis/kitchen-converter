How many grams is ⅓ cup of cocoa powder (unsweetened)?

Answer

29 g

⅓ cup of cocoa powder (unsweetened) is about 29 grams, or 1 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 86 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

Small amounts like these are hard to read on a regular kitchen scale. A pocket scale that reads to 0.1 g helps, or just use level measuring spoons.
