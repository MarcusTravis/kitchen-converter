How many grams is ⅔ cup of croutons / dry bread cubes (plain)?

Answer

20 g

⅔ cup of croutons / dry bread cubes (plain) is about 20 grams, or 0.71 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 30 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Use this for dry, unseasoned bread cubes like the ones in a bag of stuffing cubes. Seasoned cubes weigh a little more per cup.

Measuring tip

Dry crumbs and cubes are mostly air, so a cup weighs very little and pressing down changes the amount a lot. Fill the cup loosely unless the recipe says packed, or go by weight from the bag.
