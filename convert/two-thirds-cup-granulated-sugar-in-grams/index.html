How many grams is ⅔ cup of granulated sugar?

Answer

133 g

⅔ cup of granulated sugar is about 133 grams, or 4.7 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 200 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

For sticky syrups, give the cup a light coat of oil first so everything slides out and you get the full amount. Brown sugar is the one sugar that is normally packed firmly into the cup.
