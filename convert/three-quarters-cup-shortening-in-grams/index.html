How many grams is ¾ cup of vegetable shortening?

Answer

154 g

¾ cup of vegetable shortening is about 154 grams, or 5.4 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 205 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

Solid fats are easiest to measure by weight or with the markings on the wrapper. Oils and melted fats can go straight into a liquid measuring cup.
