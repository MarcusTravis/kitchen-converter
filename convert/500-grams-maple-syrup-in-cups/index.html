How many cups is 500 grams of maple syrup?

Answer

≈ 1 ⅝ cups

500 grams of maple syrup fills about 1 ⅝ cups (1.59 US cups, or about 25.4 tablespoons). The math uses USDA's figure of roughly 315 g per cup.

Measuring tip

For sticky syrups, give the cup a light coat of oil first so everything slides out and you get the full amount. Brown sugar is the one sugar that is normally packed firmly into the cup. When a recipe gives grams, a scale is always the most accurate choice.
