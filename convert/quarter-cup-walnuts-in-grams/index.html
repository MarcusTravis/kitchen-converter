How many grams is ¼ cup of walnuts (chopped)?

Answer

29 g

¼ cup of walnuts (chopped) is about 29 grams, or 1 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 117 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

Whole, chopped and sliced pieces leave different amounts of air in the cup, so the same nut can weigh quite differently depending on the cut. Pick the closest match.
