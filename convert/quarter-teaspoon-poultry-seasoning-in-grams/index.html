How many grams is ¼ teaspoon of poultry seasoning?

Answer

0.4 g

¼ teaspoon of poultry seasoning is about 0.4 grams, or 0.01 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 70.4 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

Small amounts like these are hard to read on a regular kitchen scale. A pocket scale that reads to 0.1 g helps, or just use level measuring spoons.
