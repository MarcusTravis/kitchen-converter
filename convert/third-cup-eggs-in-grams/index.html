How many grams is ⅓ cup of eggs (whole, beaten)?

Answer

81 g

⅓ cup of eggs (whole, beaten) is about 81 grams, or 2.9 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 243 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

One large egg out of the shell is roughly 50 g, so a cup holds about 5 large eggs.

Measuring tip

Liquids are the easiest thing to measure by volume, so cups and a scale will agree closely. Dairy weighs a little more or less than water depending on its fat, sugar and air content.
