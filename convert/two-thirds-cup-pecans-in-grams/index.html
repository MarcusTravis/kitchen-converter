How many grams is ⅔ cup of pecans (chopped)?

Answer

73 g

⅔ cup of pecans (chopped) is about 73 grams, or 2.6 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 109 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

Whole, chopped and sliced pieces leave different amounts of air in the cup, so the same nut can weigh quite differently depending on the cut. Pick the closest match.
