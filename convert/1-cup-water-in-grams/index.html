How many grams is 1 cup of water?

Answer

237 g

1 cup of water is about 237 grams, or 8.4 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 237 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

1 mL of water weighs very close to 1 g, which is why a cup of water is about 237 g.

Measuring tip

Liquids are the easiest thing to measure by volume, so cups and a scale will agree closely. Dairy weighs a little more or less than water depending on its fat, sugar and air content.
