How many grams is 1 cup of raisins?

Answer

145 g

1 cup of raisins is about 145 grams, or 5.1 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 145 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

Whole, chopped and sliced pieces leave different amounts of air in the cup, so the same nut can weigh quite differently depending on the cut. Pick the closest match.
