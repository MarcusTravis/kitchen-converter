How many grams is ½ cup of dried cranberries (sweetened)?

Answer

80 g

½ cup of dried cranberries (sweetened) is about 80 grams, or 2.8 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 160 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

USDA lists a quarter cup of sweetened dried cranberries at 40 g, so a full cup is about 160 g.

Measuring tip

Whole, chopped and sliced pieces leave different amounts of air in the cup, so the same nut can weigh quite differently depending on the cut. Pick the closest match.
