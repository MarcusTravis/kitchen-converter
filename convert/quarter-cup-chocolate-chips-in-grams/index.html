How many grams is ¼ cup of chocolate chips (semisweet)?

Answer

42 g

¼ cup of chocolate chips (semisweet) is about 42 grams, or 1.5 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 168 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

Small amounts like these are hard to read on a regular kitchen scale. A pocket scale that reads to 0.1 g helps, or just use level measuring spoons.
