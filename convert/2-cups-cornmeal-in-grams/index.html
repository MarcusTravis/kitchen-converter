How many grams is 2 cups of cornmeal (yellow, degermed)?

Answer

314 g

2 cups of cornmeal (yellow, degermed) is about 314 grams, or 11.1 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 157 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

Flour packs down as it sits, so a cup scooped straight from the bag can weigh noticeably more than one that was spooned in and leveled with a knife. Spoon and level for results closest to these numbers, or better, weigh it.
