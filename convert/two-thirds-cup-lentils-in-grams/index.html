How many grams is ⅔ cup of lentils (dry)?

Answer

128 g

⅔ cup of lentils (dry) is about 128 grams, or 4.5 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 192 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

These figures are for dry, uncooked grains and legumes. Once cooked they soak up water and a cup weighs very differently.
