How many grams is ½ cup of pumpkin purée (canned)?

Answer

123 g

½ cup of pumpkin purée (canned) is about 123 grams, or 4.3 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 245 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

This is plain canned pumpkin, not pumpkin pie filling (which already has sugar and spice in it). A standard 15-ounce can holds about 425 g, which is roughly 1¾ cups.

Measuring tip

Purées and sauces settle into the cup with little air, so they measure fairly consistently. Scrape the cup out with a spatula, and for whole fruit like fresh cranberries, a heaping or shaken-down cup can change the weight noticeably.
