How many grams is ¾ cup of whole milk?

Answer

183 g

¾ cup of whole milk is about 183 grams, or 6.5 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 244 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

Liquids are the easiest thing to measure by volume, so cups and a scale will agree closely. Dairy weighs a little more or less than water depending on its fat, sugar and air content.
