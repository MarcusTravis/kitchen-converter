How many grams is ¼ teaspoon of vanilla extract?

Answer

1.1 g

¼ teaspoon of vanilla extract is about 1.1 grams, or 0.04 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 208 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

Liquids are the easiest thing to measure by volume, so cups and a scale will agree closely. Dairy weighs a little more or less than water depending on its fat, sugar and air content.
