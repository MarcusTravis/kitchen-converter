How many grams is ¾ cup of quinoa (uncooked)?

Answer

128 g

¾ cup of quinoa (uncooked) is about 128 grams, or 4.5 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 170 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

These figures are for dry, uncooked grains and legumes. Once cooked they soak up water and a cup weighs very differently.
