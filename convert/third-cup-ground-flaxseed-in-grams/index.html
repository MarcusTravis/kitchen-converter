How many grams is ⅓ cup of ground flaxseed?

Answer

37 g

⅓ cup of ground flaxseed is about 37 grams, or 1.3 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 112 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

Whole, chopped and sliced pieces leave different amounts of air in the cup, so the same nut can weigh quite differently depending on the cut. Pick the closest match.
