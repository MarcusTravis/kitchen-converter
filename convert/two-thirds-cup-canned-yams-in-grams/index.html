How many grams is ⅔ cup of canned yams (sweet potatoes in syrup, drained)?

Answer

131 g

⅔ cup of canned yams (sweet potatoes in syrup, drained) is about 131 grams, or 4.6 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 196 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

In US grocery stores, canned “yams” are almost always orange sweet potatoes packed in syrup. This figure is for the drained pieces.

Measuring tip

Purées and sauces settle into the cup with little air, so they measure fairly consistently. Scrape the cup out with a spatula, and for whole fruit like fresh cranberries, a heaping or shaken-down cup can change the weight noticeably.
