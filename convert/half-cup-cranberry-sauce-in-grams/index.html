How many grams is ½ cup of cranberry sauce (canned)?

Answer

139 g

½ cup of cranberry sauce (canned) is about 139 grams, or 4.9 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 277 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

Purées and sauces settle into the cup with little air, so they measure fairly consistently. Scrape the cup out with a spatula, and for whole fruit like fresh cranberries, a heaping or shaken-down cup can change the weight noticeably.
