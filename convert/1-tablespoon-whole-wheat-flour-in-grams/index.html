How many grams is 1 tablespoon of whole wheat flour?

Answer

7.5 g

1 tablespoon of whole wheat flour is about 7.5 grams, or 0.26 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 120 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

Flour packs down as it sits, so a cup scooped straight from the bag can weigh noticeably more than one that was spooned in and leveled with a knife. Spoon and level for results closest to these numbers, or better, weigh it.
