How many grams is 2 cups of sweetened condensed milk?

Answer

612 g

2 cups of sweetened condensed milk is about 612 grams, or 21.6 ounces. That works out from USDA's figure of roughly 306 g for a full US cup (236.6 mL).

Measuring tip

Liquids are the easiest thing to measure by volume, so cups and a scale will agree closely. Dairy weighs a little more or less than water depending on its fat, sugar and air content.
