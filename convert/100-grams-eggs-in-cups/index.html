How many cups is 100 grams of eggs (whole, beaten)?

Answer

≈ ⅜ cup

100 grams of eggs (whole, beaten) fills about ⅜ cup (0.412 US cups, or about 6.58 tablespoons). The math uses USDA's figure of roughly 243 g per cup.

One large egg out of the shell is roughly 50 g, so a cup holds about 5 large eggs.

Measuring tip

Liquids are the easiest thing to measure by volume, so cups and a scale will agree closely. Dairy weighs a little more or less than water depending on its fat, sugar and air content. When a recipe gives grams, a scale is always the most accurate choice.
