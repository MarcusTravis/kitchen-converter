How the math works

To go from Fahrenheit to Celsius, subtract 32 and multiply by 5/9. To go back, multiply by 9/5 and add 32. So 350 °F is (350 − 32) × 5 ÷ 9 = 176.7 °C, which recipes usually round to 175 or 180 °C.

A fan (convection) oven blows hot air around the food, so it cooks faster at the same setting. If a recipe was written for a regular oven, start about 25 °F / 15 °C lower and check a few minutes early. If the recipe already says “fan”, use it as written.

Home ovens often run hot or cold by 15 °F or more. An inexpensive oven thermometer tells you what your oven really does.
